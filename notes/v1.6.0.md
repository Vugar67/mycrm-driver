* **📷 Scan** on My deliveries: point the camera at the QR on the bag label. Your order → marked **picked up** (the warehouse hand-over is recorded too). Someone else's bag, an order not dispatched yet or already delivered → the app tells you and records nothing.
* Scanning a bag you already picked up opens the order, so you can tap Delivered.
* Each order shows **📦 picked up HH:MM** or **bag not scanned yet**; the line above the list counts the bags still to collect.
* The app now asks for the Camera permission (needed for the scanner and, from now on, for the delivery photo too).
