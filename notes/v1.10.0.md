* **🗺 Map of your deliveries.** A new **Map** button on My deliveries shows every order on a map: the number on the marker is its place in your route, "•" = not placed yet, green = NEXT. Grey "≈" markers are approximate — only the district is known, not the exact house. The blue dot is you. Tap a marker → the order card with **Open** and **Navigate**. Orders without a map point are listed under the map.
* The map uses OpenStreetMap and works on every phone, with or without Google services. It needs the location permission (the same one as the delivery photo) for the blue dot.
