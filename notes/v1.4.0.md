* Your own delivery sequence. Tap **Sequence** on My deliveries, then tap the orders in the order you will drive them — each gets a number. Tap a numbered order again to remove its number. **Done** saves the route; orders you did not number go to the end.
* Adjust with **▲▼**: move an order one place up or down, **+ Add** puts a new order at the end of the route, **Done** when finished.
* The first order of the route is marked **NEXT** with a Navigate button. New orders that arrive during the day are marked **NEW** until you place them.
* The app warns when an URGENT order is not first or the sequence breaks delivery windows — it does not change your route.
* The dispatcher sees your numbers in Dispatch. The route is saved on the server, so it survives a phone restart.
