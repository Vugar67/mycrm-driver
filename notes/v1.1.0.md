Photo at the door.

* ✓ Delivered now opens the camera: take a photo of the package with the villa / flat number or the door visible. The photo gets a stamp (order number, time, GPS) and is sent with the order. Only the camera — not the gallery.
* No photo possible? "Can't take photo" + a reason (customer refused, handed over in person, camera not working, other). The dispatcher sees the reason.
* Could not deliver: "Photo + return" (optional photo of the closed door / wrong address) or "Return without photo".
* No internet at the door: the photo stays on the phone — tap "Try again", no need to retake.
* Android asks once for location permission (used only at the moment of the photo).
