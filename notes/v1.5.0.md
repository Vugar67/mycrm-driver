* **Trips.** The dispatcher can now send you on a trip: **Pick up** (bring staff from their homes), **Drop off** (take staff home) or a **Task**. Trips appear at the top of **My deliveries**, above your orders.
* A trip card shows the time, every stop with the people to pick up or drop off there, a **🧭 Navigate** button for each stop that has a map link, and the dispatcher's note.
* When the whole trip is finished, tap **✓ Done** once. Made a mistake? **↩ Undo** is on the **Done** tab for 5 minutes.
* A trip from an earlier day that is still open is marked **overdue** — call the dispatcher.
